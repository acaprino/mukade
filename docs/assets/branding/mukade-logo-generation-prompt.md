# Generation prompt — Mukade / Musashi logo

Original image kept in: [`mukade-logo-oni.png`](mukade-logo-oni.png)

## Prompt

Create a horizontal vector logo, on a fully transparent background, for the brand **MUKADE**. Panoramic composition of about **2000 × 689 px**, **2.9:1** format, with generous transparent space around the elements.

On the left place **Musashi**, an original cyberpunk character inspired by a biomechanical centipede armour: figure shown in three-quarter view, from the chest up, dark armoured helmet with glowing orange details, luminous cyan eyes, mechanical respirator, black and graphite plates, cables and metal parts. Behind the shoulders, three long sharp blades or antennas, black with orange edges, pointing diagonally upwards. Highly detailed cel-shaded sci-fi illustration style, marked black outlines, metallic surfaces, volumes that are sharp and readable at small sizes too. The character must blend visually with the initial letter without covering the rest of the mark.

On the right write only the word **MUKADE**, in capitals, with a **custom typographic logotype**: geometric sans-serif font, extra-bold, extended horizontally, strongly slanted in italics to the right, low and wide letters, aggressive diagonal cuts, pointed corners and arrow terminals. The initial M is large and slender; the A has a sharp inner triangle; the final E has sharp horizontal cuts. Do not use a serif, rounded or calligraphic font. As a style reference, not as a font to identify: futuristic cyberpunk/racing logo lettering, close in construction to extended geometric typefaces but redrawn to measure.

Colouring of the lettering: night-blue fill that is almost black, with gradients from blue-black to electric blue and inner metallic highlights. Main outline in a very light blue-white, thick and sharp; a second outer outline in blue-black to separate the logo from the background. Add inner cyan and electric blue glows concentrated mostly around the D and the A, with small points of light like flares, without reducing legibility. Light and controlled blue outer glow, never a diffuse blur of the whole word.

Under the whole word place a long sharp horizontal line, made of several parallel white-cyan and electric blue bands, with pointed ends; the line follows the dynamic slant of the mark and ends in a graphic tail integrated with the letters. Visual balance: the character takes about the first quarter of the width, the lettering dominates the remaining three quarters.

Clean and professional final rendering, precise vector edges, high contrast, real alpha transparency, no background, no rectangular drop shadow, no watermark, no additional text.

## Negative prompt

white or black background, opaque background, watermark, additional writing, wrong text, missing letters, more than one word, serif font, rounded font, thin lettering, excessive glow, blurred image, low resolution, photographic realistic character, full-body character, several characters, square composition, frame, badge, circle, mockup, reflective surface under the logo

## Technical notes

- **Text:** `MUKADE`, exactly six capital letters.
- **Font:** custom logotype; an installable font cannot be determined with certainty from the original image alone. For a manual reconstruction, start from an extended/extra-bold geometric sans-serif and apply mechanical italics, horizontal expansion and a vector redraw of the terminals.
- **Palette:** blue-black `#050A16`, night blue `#0B1930`, electric blue `#2457FF`, cyan `#70E9FF`, ice white `#EAFBFF`, glowing orange `#FF641E`.
- **Effects:** double outline, inner metallic gradient, localized blue/cyan glow and small luminous flares.
- **Recommended output:** transparent RGBA PNG, 2000 px wide; for production, rebuild a vector SVG version too.
